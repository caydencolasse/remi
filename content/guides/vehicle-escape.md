How to get out of a vehicle when the doors will not open, including a vehicle in water.

## Steps

1. Unbuckle your seatbelt. If it is jammed, cut it with a seatbelt cutter.
2. Open a side window. If it will not open, break it at a lower corner with a window-breaking tool.
3. Get children out first, then yourself, through the window.

## Notes

- In water, act at once. Do not wait for the car to fill and do not stop to call for help until you are out.
- Break a side window, not the windshield. Windshields are laminated and much harder to break.
- Some newer cars have laminated side windows too. Check your owner's manual now so you know which windows can be broken.
- Keep an escape tool within reach of the driver's seat, on your keys or the visor, not in the glovebox or the trunk.
- Once out, move away from the vehicle and then call 911.
