What to do in the first hour of a power outage at home, and how to get through the night.

## Steps

1. Use a flashlight to move around. Use your phone light only to find one.
2. Fill a few containers with tap water while the taps still run.
3. Find a power bank, or a charged laptop you can plug your phone into.
4. Turn on Low Power Mode on your phone and close apps you are not using.
5. Keep the fridge and freezer doors shut.
6. Unplug computers and TVs so a surge does not damage them when power returns.
7. Leave one light switched on so you know when the power is back.
8. Check on neighbours who are elderly or live alone, if it is safe to go out.

## Notes

- Prefer flashlights to candles. If you use candles, keep them away from anything that burns and never leave them lit when you sleep.
- Never run a generator, camp stove or grill indoors or in a garage. They produce carbon monoxide, which you cannot see or smell.
- If someone at home depends on powered medical equipment, treat the outage as urgent and call for help early.
- If it is late and everyone is safe, the best next step is often to sleep and check again in the morning.
