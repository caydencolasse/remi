What to keep in a car so you can get out, get seen and get through a wait.

## Steps

1. Within reach of the driver: a seatbelt cutter and window breaker.
2. In the glovebox: a flashlight, a phone charging cable and a power bank.
3. In the trunk: a first-aid kit, warning triangles or a beacon, and jumper cables or a jump starter.
4. For waiting: water, a blanket, and snacks that keep.
5. For winter: an ice scraper, gloves, a warm hat and a small shovel.
6. Check the kit twice a year. Replace water, test the flashlight and recharge the power bank.

## Notes

- A kit only helps if you can reach it. The escape tool belongs by the driver, not in the trunk.
- Add anything your household needs: medication, baby supplies, pet supplies.
